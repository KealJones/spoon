# spoon-adapt

Evidence-gated learning, adaptation, reconciliation, promotion, and
contradiction refinement.

## Owns

- Narrow correction policy and mutation authorization.
- CAS-checked graph reconciliation without deleting history.
- Contradiction records, demonstrated scope refinements, and inherited uncertainty.
- Skill discovery, promotion gates, compression, and retirement evidence.

## How it works with the system

`spoon-credit` supplies attribution evidence and `spoon-engine` supplies the
trusted episode boundary and mutation authority. `spoon-graph` applies the
versioned changes. The crate is deliberately conservative: weak or simulated
evidence can schedule work, but cannot silently promote or rewrite knowledge.
